Portfolio site for **Bijoya Deb** (Bee-joy-ah, "Bee"), a product designer based in Toronto, ON.

Live site: add your GitHub Pages link here once deployed (e.g. `designwithbee.co`).

## What's on the site

- **Featured Case Studies** — four projects presented as expandable case studies with search and tag filtering:
- **Chitrok** — Rebuilding e-commerce marketplace for South Asian creatives (Lead AI Engineer and Product Designer)
- **BrewBase** — a Toronto café discovery app, designed around the kind of day you're planning (Co-Founder & Product Designer)
- **MyManagement Portal** — BBA Students Central Hub for Resources, Career and Program Pathways (UTSC, Department of Management)
- **U of T Public Health** — design system and information architecture across 33 department sites (Dalla Lana School of Public Health)
- **Experience** — résumé timeline with the tools and skills tagged under each role, plus a "Tools I use" rail: click a tool to jump to the work that used it. A downloadable résumé (PDF) is linked from the header and the Experience section.
- **Photography** — "Selected frames," a preview collage of outdoor and commercial photography (full gallery on VSCO, coming soon).
- **Illustrations** — a separate gallery of illustration and branding work, including the Potions & Provisions chalk sets, BrewBase café branding, Toronto Flower Market postcards, and Cool Girl Literature blog branding.
- **Community** — GTA art clubs, Notion Toronto Community, Toronto Humane Society (Feline Behaviour Specialist), and more.
- **Contact** — a postcard-style form that sends messages straight to Bijoya's email inbox, plus email, LinkedIn, and Instagram links.

## Tech

- Plain static **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** — no build step, no framework, nothing to install.
- Dark/light mode toggle, collapsible case-study drawers, live search + hashtag filters, and a tool-based jump navigation in Experience.
- **Recruiter summary** — a floating "Hiring? 30-sec summary" button (or press `R`) opens a slide-over with role targets, impact numbers, toolkit, case-study links, résumé download and email copy.
- **Quick jump** — `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` (or `/`) opens a command palette to jump to any case study or section, download the résumé, copy the email, or switch theme.
- **In-page image viewer** — screenshots and illustrations open in a lightbox with ← → keys, swipe on mobile, and an "Open full size" link.
- Scroll progress bar, reveal-on-scroll, count-up impact stats, a typed role line in the hero, cursor spotlight on cards and a back-to-top button — all switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- Theme choice is remembered between visits; Open Graph tags give LinkedIn/Slack link previews.
- Contact form sends through [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com) (free plan: 250 messages a month, no account needed). Paste your access key into `WEB3FORMS_KEY` in `index.html`. Until a key is set, it falls back to [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co). A hidden spam trap and a one-minute cooldown per visitor protect the monthly allowance.
- All illustrations and photography on the site are Bijoya's own work; the BrewBase illustration set was hand-drawn by her.
